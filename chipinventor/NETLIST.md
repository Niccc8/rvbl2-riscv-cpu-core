# Wiring checklist

GENERATED by `scripts/gen_docs.py` from `ci_top.v` and `blocks/*.v` - do not hand-edit.

Every row below is one wire to draw on the ChipInventor canvas. Tick them off as you go;
the count at the bottom is what a complete project has.


## Before you start

- Name the two input pins exactly **`clk_i`** and **`rst_i`**. The testbench instantiates
  the project as `top dut (.clk_i(clk), .rst_i(rst));` and will not compile otherwise.

- The canvas **cannot slice a bus**. Every wire connects a whole port to a whole port of
  the same width. That is why `ir_fields` exists, and why `funct3` comes from
  `control_unit.op_size` rather than from the instruction register.

- **`lsu`'s port names read backwards.** `mem_data_o` is an **input** and `core_data_i` is an
  **output**. This is the easiest wire on the whole canvas to get wrong.

- Set `dmem`'s `DMEM_WORDS` parameter to **2048** on its instance. Leaving it blank still
  gives 2048 (the block's default), but setting it explicitly makes the resize knob visible.
