> That is the order the official validation firmware uses (`crcb s0, s1, s0`).

> here - but do not "helpfully" swap the two wires.

> `DMEM_WORDS` is the resize knob. 2048 is the full 8 kB (Table 6/13) and 65,536
> flip-flops. If place-and-route cannot absorb that, 1024 or 512 works with no other
> change anywhere: the block reads 0 and writes nothing above the instantiated array,
> which Block Guide 4.3 explicitly permits.
